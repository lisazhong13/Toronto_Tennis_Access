User
I am working on a paper using Open Data Toronto. I am considering the
Tennis Courts dataset. What could be an interesting story to tell with
this dataset?

ChatGPT
Suggested focusing on differences in Toronto's City-owned tennis
infrastructure rather than reporting only a city-wide court total.
Possible dimensions included Public versus Club operation, lighting,
winter-play designation, number of courts, and geographic distribution.
The analysis could also combine tennis facility records with
neighbourhood population to compare reported courts per resident.


User
I want to look at whether tennis access differs across Toronto and
whether Public and Club courts are different.

ChatGPT
Suggested using the Tennis Courts – Facilities dataset as the primary
dataset and combining it with Toronto neighbourhood boundaries and
Neighbourhood Profiles. Proposed examining operation type, lights,
winter-play designation, reported court count, location, and courts per
10,000 residents.


============================================================
DATA CLEANING AND ANALYSIS
============================================================

User
There are 173 rows, but some locations appear more than once as Public
and Club. How should I deal with this?

ChatGPT
Recommended distinguishing between a "record", a "location", and a
physical court. A row should be described as a tennis record rather than
automatically treated as a unique physical facility. Locations represented
by both Public and Club records should not simply be deleted because the
rows describe different operating arrangements. Any sum of the court-count
variable should therefore be described as "reported courts" unless the
physical facilities have been independently deduplicated.


User
The WinterPlay variable contains Yes and blank values. Should I treat
the blanks as No?

ChatGPT
Recommended not interpreting a blank as No. A blank only shows that a
winter-play designation was not recorded. Suggested recoding blank entries
as "Not indicated" and explaining this measurement limitation in the paper.


User
How should I calculate tennis court supply by neighbourhood?

ChatGPT
Suggested spatially joining each tennis record to Toronto's neighbourhood
boundaries, summing reported courts within each neighbourhood, joining
2021 Census population, and calculating:

reported courts per 10,000 residents =
(reported courts / neighbourhood population) * 10,000

Also recommended retaining neighbourhoods with no tennis records and
assigning them zero reported courts.

User
What figures should I include?

ChatGPT
Suggested complementary figures showing:
1. the distribution of reported courts per tennis record;
2. the geographic distribution of Public and Club records;
3. the share of Public and Club records with lights;
4. the share with a winter-play designation;
5. neighbourhood population versus reported courts; and
6. the distribution of reported courts per 10,000 residents.

Recommended using descriptive figure titles that state the main finding
rather than titles such as "Histogram of...".
